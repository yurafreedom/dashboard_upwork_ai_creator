**Share an example where an AI tool distorted a product (logo, frame, hardware, etc.). How did you catch it and how did you fix it?**

In an independent Eligion Labs packaging study, the generator printed the brand name lengthwise along a sachet. I compared it with the original, where the name runs across the short edge. I isolated the print area, used the original layout as the reference and checked the text direction and weight marking again before using the frame.

**Walk us through your full workflow from reference photos to a final, product-accurate lifestyle image. Which tools do you use at each step?**

I start with the client’s reference photos and identify visible hardware, seams, markings, color and scale. I use Higgsfield to build the scene, then compare the output with the references side by side. I correct the affected area or keep the original product pixels where possible. I can demonstrate this on a single SKU. My samples document this workflow, and I would not claim advanced Photoshop experience on their basis.

**How do you keep a product consistent across multiple images or video frames, including angle, scale, and color?**

I use one approved product view per supported angle, keep scale and color references next to the working frame, and check the first, middle and final frames. I also scrub through transitions to catch moving text or hardware. If a requested angle shows an unseen part, I ask for another reference.

**What is your typical turnaround for one lifestyle image, one studio image alteration, and one 15 second product video?**

For planning, I’d allow 1–2 business days for a first lifestyle image, half a day to one day for a straightforward studio alteration, and 3–5 business days for a 15-second edit once the references and shot list are approved. These are estimates. A complex reconstruction would need a separate estimate.

**Which AI tools do you use, and do their terms allow full commercial use and transfer of rights to the client?**

I use Higgsfield for image and video work. Before committing to a commercial handover I would confirm the specific model and plan terms for the requested use, and that the supplied photography, logos, and other assets are cleared. I would list the assets and usage terms in the delivery.
